Privacy and visit counting

This page explains what this site records about your visits, and how you choose whether it may. In short: your answers and your progress never leave your device, and your visits are counted only if you allow it.

What stays on your device

Everything you do on this site stays in your own browser. When you mark a morning or an afternoon as done, or finish a play, the site saves that record in your browser's local storage. The same is true of anything you type into a demo. None of it is sent anywhere, and nobody else can see it, including your lecturer.

What is counted, if you allow it

With your permission, the site uses Google Analytics, through Google Tag Manager, to count visits. It records:

  • which pages are opened, and how long they stay open;
  • the kind of device and browser, and the screen size;
  • the page or site that sent you here;
  • an approximate location (country and city), which Google works out from your internet address.

It never records your answers, your progress, your name, your index number or anything you type. The counts help your lecturer see which pages and plays students use, so that the module can be improved. Google keeps the counts under its own privacy policy, and stores a cookie whose name starts with _ga to recognise a returning browser.

Your choice

The first time you open the site, a bar at the bottom of the page asks whether your visit may be counted. Until you press Allow, nothing is sent to Google, and Google Tag Manager is not even loaded. If you press Do not allow, it never is. If your browser sends the Global Privacy Control signal, the site treats that as Do not allow and does not ask.

Your choice is saved in this browser only. You can change it at any time with the buttons below, on the portal's copy of this page:

Turn on JavaScript to see and change your choice.

Note

Choosing Do not allow later also removes the site's _ga cookies from this browser.

Copies of this site

If you open a copy of this site from your own computer, or from a clone of its repository, nothing is counted and nothing is asked.
