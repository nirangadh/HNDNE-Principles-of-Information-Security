# Evidence pack template

This template is the shape of every evidence pack you submit after Days 2 to 6. Copy it, fill it in, and submit the whole pack as one PDF, as the [evidence packs brief](../coursework/evidence-packs-brief.md) explains. The day's lab sheet says exactly what goes into each part.

| | |
|---|---|
| Day | 2, 3, 4, 5 or 6 |
| Index number | your index number, written without spaces or hyphens |

## 1. Your personal values

For each value the lab sheet asks for, give the step it comes from, the value itself, and how you got it. Values made from someone else's index number, or from the example in the lab sheet, score zero for this part.

## 2. Your concept sketch

Draw the day's mechanism by hand, on paper, and label every part the lab sheet names. Photograph the drawing and add the photograph here, or as a page after this form with its page number here. Neat drawing is not marked; correct labels are. A sketch drawn on a computer, or copied from a slide, scores zero for this part.

## 3. Your explanation

Answer the lab sheet's one "why" question in your own words, in no more than 120 words. Simple English is fine: the ideas are marked, not the language.

## Before you submit

- Every item the lab sheet lists is present and labelled with its step.
- Every value comes from your own index number, and you show how you got it.
- The sketch is drawn by hand, photographed, and every named part is labelled.
- The explanation is your own, answers the "why", and is no more than 120 words.
