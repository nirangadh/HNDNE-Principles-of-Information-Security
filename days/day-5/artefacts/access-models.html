Who opens what: three access-control models

Authorisation needs rules: who may do what to which resource. An access-control model is the way those rules are set. Choose a model, try its action, and watch which Taxila records open.

Step 1

Choose a model

Discretionary access control

The owner decides

The owner of each resource decides who may use it. lecturer01 owns the folder of module 2 marks.

ResourceOwnerWho may open it

Flexible: the owner can share at once, with nobody else involved.

Mandatory access control

The system's labels decide

Every resource carries a label, and every account a clearance. An account opens a resource only if its clearance is at least the label. Nobody, not even an owner, can override this.

ResourceLabelWho may open it

Clearances: registrar restricted, lecturer01 confidential, student01 internal.

Role-based access control

Roles carry permissions; people are assigned to roles

Choose a person, then assign them a role. Their permissions change at once, because the permissions belong to the role, not to the person.
