Taxila College: facts for the business impact analysis
=======================================================

Collected by the Head of IT (information technology) and the finance office
for the trainee security analyst. Taxila College is fictional; these figures
belong to the teaching scenario only.

1. The fee payment page, pay.taxila.example
-------------------------------------------
- On an ordinary evening, about 20 students pay their fees in an hour.
- On the last evening before a fee deadline, about 600 students pay in each
  hour between 6 pm and midnight.
- After the deadline, every student who has not paid is charged a late fee.
- On the dead deadline night last semester the page stopped for three hours.
  Hundreds of students missed the deadline, and the finance office spent a
  week sorting out late fees.
- The bank's payment gateway takes the card payment. Taxila never stores
  card numbers. The bank keeps its own record of every payment, and sends
  the finance office a statement each morning.
- When a payment succeeds, the page updates the student's fee balance in the
  Student Information System (SIS). If the page cannot reach the SIS, it
  cannot take payments.
- The finance office can take payments at its counter during office hours.
  It cannot do so in the evening.

2. The Student Information System, sis.taxila.example
-----------------------------------------------------
- The Registrar's office, every lecturer and every student use it on every
  working day. The Kandy campus reaches it over the internet.
- It holds student records, grades, fee balances and national identity card
  numbers.
- In marking weeks, lecturers enter about 1,200 grades on each working day.
  Lecturers keep their own marking sheets, but re-entering grades takes time
  and causes mistakes.
- Results are released on a fixed day each semester. Employers and other
  universities check transcripts against the SIS.
- The fee payment page, the Learning Management System's class lists and
  the examinations office all read from the SIS.

3. Backups today
----------------
- Once a week, at the weekend, the Head of IT copies the SIS database and
  the fee records to one external disk.
- The disk stays on a shelf in the server room, next to the server.
- Nobody has ever restored the SIS from this disk.

4. What the Head of IT has to work with
---------------------------------------
- One spare server, switched off, in the server room. It is powerful enough
  to run the SIS.
- The small rack at the Kandy campus has space for one more disk.
- A team of four. No changes to production systems during exam weeks.
