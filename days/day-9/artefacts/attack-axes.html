Three questions for any attack

Any attack can be described by three questions. Does the attacker only watch, or act? Do they start inside the college, or outside? Did they choose Taxila, or take whoever was open? The three answers give the attack's profile, and the profile points to the controls that fit.

How to use it

For each event, answer the three questions with the switches, then choose Check. The page tells you which answers fit, and why. Nothing on this page reaches a network: every event is a prepared description.

The events come from Taxila's story and from Days 7 and 8. The profiles are the ones a defender would write down; some events could be argued another way, and the explanation says why this answer fits best.
