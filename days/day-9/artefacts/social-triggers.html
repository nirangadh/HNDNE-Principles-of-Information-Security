Which button does it press?

Social engineering attacks people, not systems. Each approach presses a human button, a trigger, so that the person acts before they think. Name the trigger, and the approach loses most of its power.

The six triggers
TriggerWhat the message makes you feel
Authorityan important person is asking
Urgencythere is no time to check
Fearsomething bad will happen if you do not act
Rewardyou will gain something
Curiosityyou want to see what it is
Helpfulnessa polite person needs your help
Six approaches to Taxila's staff

Read each one. Choose the trigger it pulls hardest. Many pull more than one; the answer explains the others.
