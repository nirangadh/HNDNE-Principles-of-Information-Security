Four ways to treat a risk

Every risk on a register needs a treatment. You can lower it with a control, stop the activity that creates it, pass its cost to someone else, or live with it as a recorded choice. Whatever you choose, something usually remains.

How to use it

Each risk shows its score, likelihood times impact, on Taxila's scale of 1 to 25. Choose one of the four treatments and read what remains: the residual risk. Some choices fit better than others, and an accepted risk needs an owner.

The risks and scores are prepared examples from Taxila's story. Real scores come from your own register this afternoon.
