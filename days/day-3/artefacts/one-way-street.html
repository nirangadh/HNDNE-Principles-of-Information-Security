Step 3

The only way back is to guess

Without a way back, someone with a digest can only guess: hash a likely input and see whether the digest matches. Here is a list of twenty guesses. Try it on two digests.

    Step 4

    Two inputs, one digest?

    A collision is two different inputs with the same digest. Hash twenty thousand different inputs and find the two digests that look most alike.

    Step 5

    Older fingerprints

    Hash functions are retired once collisions can be found for them. Here is the word Taxila through three of them.

    This page uses your browser's own cryptography, called Web Crypto, for the Advanced Encryption Standard (AES), SHA-1 and SHA-256. Web Crypto does not offer MD5 at all, because MD5 is no longer trusted, so its digest here was computed in advance. Every guess on this page is from the page's own list; nothing you do leaves the page.
