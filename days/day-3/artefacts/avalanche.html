One change, a new fingerprint

A good hash function has the avalanche effect: change one character of the input, and the digest changes completely. Here are two lines of a results file, one mark apart. Edit either line, or press the button to change one character, and compare the two SHA-256 digests.

This page uses your browser's own cryptography, called Web Crypto, to compute each digest. What you type stays on this page. A space is a character too: add one at the end of a line, and its size grows by one byte and its digest changes completely.
