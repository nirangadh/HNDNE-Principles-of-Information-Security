A keyed seal

Anyone can compute a digest, including someone who changed the file. A hash-based message authentication code (HMAC) mixes a secret key into the hash, so only a key-holder can make or check the seal. Here the examinations office seals a results file, and both its Colombo and Kandy offices hold the key.
