Which job does it do?

LO2LO3

Fourteen tools and habits, at Taxila and in other places. Sort each one by the job it really does: hide the content, show a change, show a change and that a key-holder made it, or protect nothing.

How to play
  1. Choose a job for each card. You can switch the timer off before you start.
  2. Then decide between pairs that look alike. Every answer shows its reason.
