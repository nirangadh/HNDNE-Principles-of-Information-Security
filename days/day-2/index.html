TAXILA TODAY

A paper that must travel

Last semester a draft exam paper leaked from the store. The Registrar has a new rule: from today, every paper must be unreadable to anyone without the key, even if the folder leaks again. Then the Head of IT adds a problem. The Kandy campus prints its own copies. How do you get the key to Kandy without anyone reading it on the way?
