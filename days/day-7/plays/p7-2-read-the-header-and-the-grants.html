Read the header and the grants

LO4

The Head of IT asks you to read three exhibits: the header of a quarantined message, the grants on the SIS database, and the settings for its data. Some lines are fine and some are not. Find the faults, then choose how to deal with each one.

How to play
  1. Press a line to flag it, press again to clear it. Then check your flags.
  2. For each fault you find, choose the fix. A correct line flagged by mistake costs a point.
