Grants on Taxila's SIS (Student Information System) database, taxila-db
Teaching copy for the Day 7 lab, floor tier. Every account and value is a prepared sample.

The Head of IT (information technology) ran SHOW GRANTS for each database account and
saved the output. Part A is what the Head of IT found. Part B is the same server after the fix.

What each account is for:
  taxila-portal  the account the student and lecturer portal uses. It reads student
                 records and grades, and enters and corrects grades.
  registrar      the Registrar's reporting account. It reads results to print reports.
  itadmin        the Head of IT's administration account. It is used only to look
                 after the server: making accounts, backups and changes to tables.
  lecturer01     an old account from before the portal, when lecturers typed grades
                 straight into the database. Lecturers now use the portal.

For each line in Part A, mark:
  KEEP    the account needs this to do its job
  REMOVE  the account does not need this
and write the line that should replace it, if any.
