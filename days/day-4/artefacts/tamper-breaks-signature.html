One edit, a broken signature

The Registrar has signed a transcript. An employer checks it with the Registrar's public key. Make one small change and check again: the digest changes completely, so the signature no longer matches.

Change one thing, then let the employer check

Each button makes one change to the employer's copy, then checks the Registrar's signature with the Registrar's public key.

Step 3

Sign the changed copy with another key

Someone who changed the grade cannot use the Registrar's private key. Suppose they sign the changed copy with a key of their own.

This page uses your browser's own cryptography, called Web Crypto, for SHA-256 and for signatures with the Rivest, Shamir and Adleman method (RSA). Notice that the transcript stays readable at every step: a signature shows a change, but hides nothing.
