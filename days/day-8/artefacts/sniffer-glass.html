× Plain web

✓ Secure web

The index number is in no packet. The watcher still sees who talked to whom, on which port, when, and how much.

Both captures were made on one machine, so both ends show the address 127.0.0.1. On the student Wi-Fi the two addresses would be a student's laptop and the server.
