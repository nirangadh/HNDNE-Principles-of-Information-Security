Check the risk rows

LO1LO5

Another trainee wrote Taxila's first risk rows. Some are right and some are not. Find the faulty rows, then choose how to fix each one.

How to play
  1. Press a row to flag it, press again to clear it. Then check your flags.
  2. For each fault you find, choose the fix. A correct row flagged by mistake costs a point.
