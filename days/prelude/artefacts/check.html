Check your laptop

This page tests your browser for what the module's demos need. It runs by itself when the page opens, and it sends nothing anywhere: every test happens inside your browser. Open it twice, once from the portal online and once from your offline copy.

The checks

    Result Checking...

    The checks take a second or two.

    What the checks mean

    Required checks decide the result. If one fails, the demos will not work in this browser, and the check says what to do. Helpful checks do not decide the result: the demos still work, a little less well.

    The two cryptography tests ask your browser's own cryptography, called Web Crypto, to work out two results whose correct answers are published. You will learn what these operations are on Days 2 and 3. Here they only prove that the tool is there and gives the right answers.
