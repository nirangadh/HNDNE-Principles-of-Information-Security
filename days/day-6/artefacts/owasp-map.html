The OWASP map: ten web risks, three for today

The Open Worldwide Application Security Project (OWASP) keeps a list of the ten most common risks to web applications, called the OWASP Top 10. It is a map: it shows where flaws are usually found. This page shows the edition current at the time of writing. Open each view to see where today's ideas sit, and where earlier days already took you.

The names are OWASP's own. The short lines in plain English are this module's, not OWASP's wording. Each entry on OWASP's own site has a page with examples and fixes. Nothing on this page leaves your browser.
