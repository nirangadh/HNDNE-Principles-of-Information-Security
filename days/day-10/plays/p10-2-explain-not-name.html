Explain it, do not just name it

LO1LO3LO6

A classmate drafted three answers to questions set somewhere other than Taxila. Some lines explain well. Others only name a mechanism, or use a word from the wrong list. Find those lines, then choose a better one.

How to play
  1. Press a line to flag it, press again to clear it. Then check your flags.
  2. For each weak line you find, choose the better line. A good line flagged by mistake costs a point.
