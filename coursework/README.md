# Coursework

Two pieces of coursework run alongside the teaching days, both built from what you do in the afternoons and both set at Lumora College.

| Coursework | Weight | Individual or group | Brief |
|---|---|---|---|
| Individual evidence packs | 25% of the module | Individual | [Evidence packs brief](evidence-packs-brief.md) |
| Group tabletop exercise | 25% of the module | Group of four (three or five where the cohort needs it) | [Tabletop brief](tabletop-brief.md) |

Dates and submissions are announced for your cohort.

## Individual evidence packs

Five packs, one after each of Days 2 to 6, each due before the next teaching day. Each pack holds your personal values from the day's lab, a concept sketch you draw by hand, and a short explanation in your own words.

Read the [evidence packs brief](evidence-packs-brief.md).

## Group tabletop exercise

Your group is Lumora's security team. You prepare a risk register and a controls map across Days 6 to 9, handle a live incident on Day 10 morning, and then submit an after-action report and a continuity plan.

Read the [tabletop brief](tabletop-brief.md). It is available from the start of the module, and groups form on Day 5.
